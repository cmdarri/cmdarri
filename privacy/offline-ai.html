1. Overview

OfflineAI is a local, on-device AI assistant application. The app is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account or registration required
  • No tracking, analytics, or telemetry
  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No cloud servers
  • No social media integration

3. How your data is handled

All of your data is processed on your device only:

  • Chats and messages are stored locally in an on-device database (Room).
  • AI responses are generated locally using an AI model running on your device (llama.cpp). Your messages are never sent to any server.
  • App preferences are stored locally (DataStore).

4. What data leaves your device

The only time data is transmitted is when you manually download an AI model inside the app. In that case:

  • Your device connects to the model hosting service (e.g. Hugging Face) to download the model file.
  • Only the model file is downloaded; no personal data is sent.

5. Permissions we use

PermissionWhy it is used
Internet Used only to download AI model files you choose to install.
Notifications Used to show progress while a response is being generated or a model is downloading.

6. Children’s privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated “Last updated” date.

8. Contact
