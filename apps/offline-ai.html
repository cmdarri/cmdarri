CmDarri - Offline AI

On-device AI assistant

Private offline AI. Chat without internet after downloading a model.

Overview

What it does

CmDarri - Offline AI runs an AI model directly on your phone. Download the model of your choice once, then ask questions, brainstorm ideas and get help writing with no connection at all — useful on flights, in remote areas, or anywhere you would rather not be online.

The model runs locally through llama.cpp, so a reply is generated on the handset rather than on a remote server. Chats, notes and settings are kept in on-device storage, and there is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Inside the app the package is listed as OfflineAI, with the application id com.oa. It is the same application.

Features

Key capabilities

  • 01

    Local AI chat

    Ask questions, brainstorm and get help writing. Responses are generated on the device by an AI model running through llama.cpp — messages are never sent to a server.

  • 02

    Multiple models

    Download and switch between models to balance speed and capability. Smaller models respond faster on older devices; larger models give better answers.

  • 03

    Built-in notes

    Keep and manage notes alongside your chats, in a dedicated Notes tab.

  • 04

    Works offline

    Once the model is installed the app needs no internet connection at all.

  • 05

    Tunable engine

    Adjust default context length, default temperature, CPU threads and GPU acceleration, and turn streaming responses or auto-load on start on or off.

  • 06

    Conversation management

    Keep multiple named conversations, set a maximum conversation count, and start a new chat at any time.
